Keyboard reference

How to type

Type the Roman spelling. Nepali appears in the editor as you go. These examples show the keys the prototype currently understands.

01 / Start typing

Words appear directly

Type a word without opening a candidate list. Press Space to finish it and start the next one.

namaste→नमस्ते
nepal→नेपाल

While a word is active, Backspace removes the last Roman key and updates the Nepali result.

02 / Vowels

Length changes the word

Consonants start full: k and ka both give क. A vowel changes the sound: ki gives कि. Use aa for long आ, as in का. ii or ee make sounds like की; uu or oo make sounds like कू.

pani→पनि
paani→पानी
k→क
ka→क
kaa→का
03 / Half sounds

Full consonants, automatic joins

Adjacent consonants join automatically, with the last consonant full: kr and kra give क्र. A vowel after a consonant keeps the syllables separate: kar gives कर. A known word can use its listed spelling.

kr→क्र
kra→क्र
kri→क्रि
shakti→शक्ति

Type ` for an explicit half consonant: k` or ka` → क्, and kr` → क्र्. Space and punctuation keep that half form. A following vowel starts independently: k`i → क्इ. The / shortcut works the same way.

Use `= or /= to add a joiner after the halant: par`=yo → पर्‍यो. The font and browser determine the visible shape. Choose Strict half above to keep the earlier phonetic behavior, where k → क् and kr → क्र् until you add a vowel. Changing the setting preserves existing text.

04 / Ch and Chh sounds

च or छ

Use ch for च and chh for the aspirated छ. Both start full; a backtick makes them half:

KeysDefault result
chच
chaच
chhछ
chhaछ

cha offers छ as an alternative in the readings dropdown. Type chha to get छ directly. Listed complete words can keep their own spelling, such as huncha → हुन्छ.

05 / R and Y forms

र्य or र्‍य

Ordinary ry forms र्य, as in kaarya → कार्य, suurya → सूर्य, saundarya → सौन्दर्य, and aachaarya → आचार्य. These complete listed words automatically use र्‍य:

paryo→पर्‍यो
garyo→गर्‍यो
maryo→मर्‍यो
maaryo→मार्‍यो
bharyo→भर्‍यो
taryo→तर्‍यो
saryo→सर्‍यो

maryo and maaryo keep their different vowels. Incidental title case works: Garyo → गर्‍यो. Reserved Shift keys select their sounds: Saryo → षर्यो. These are whole-word entries; adding a suffix does not automatically reuse them.

06 / Listed word shortcuts

Listed shortcuts and spellings

The word list accepts these shortcuts and their explicit long-vowel spellings:

puryaunu→पुर्‍याउनु
puryaaunu→पुर्‍याउनु
bharyang→भर्‍याङ
bharyaanga→भर्‍याङ

Only the listed puryaunu shortcut supplies long आ followed by उ where you typed au. In ordinary phonetic conversion, au still means औ: kau → कौ.

The listed bharyang shortcut fills in long आ and chooses the र्‍य form. Keep the complete listed spelling bharyaanga to receive that same joiner preference. In Full mode, the explicit form bhar`=yaang also gives भर्‍याङ without a final a.

Four other complete spellings supply reviewed vowels or nasal marks: halyo → हाल्यो, nabhani → नभनी, gaunle → गाउँले, and dindaina → दिँदैन. These exact entries do not change ordinary vowel, Shift, half-consonant or explicit-mark keys, and they do not infer attached native words.

This repository demo also includes the unreleased native spelling update. Try these complete lowercase keys:

imandar→इमान्दार
sarasar→सरासर
sakos→सकोस्
kathanak→कथानक
arambha→आरम्भ
ekadhik→एकाधिक
jaghanya→जघन्य
pukar→पुकार
niskanda→निस्कँदा
bora→बोरा
utthan→उत्थान
samanjasya→सामञ्जस्य

Type bhagna to display भग्न, then choose भाग्न from the readings dropdown or press Alt + 2 while the word is active. Space keeps the displayed choice. These distinct readings need your intended meaning; their order preserves the earlier default.

The later unreleased native-form batch also accepts complete keys such as astitwalai → अस्तित्वलाई, karyaharuma → कार्यहरूमा, and pairaheka → पाइरहेका. Type mahila for महिला with माहिला as an alternative, shanta for शान्त with शान्ता, or angrejharuko for अङ्ग्रेजहरूको with अंग्रेजहरूको. Choose the second reading from the dropdown or use Alt + 2. See the complete 34-key guide and source review. These whole keys work in either consonant setting; other native endings are not inferred.

For an unlisted spelling, request र्‍य with /= and type the vowels yourself. Examples: gar/=yo → गर्‍यो, pur/=yaaunu → पुर्‍याउनु, and bhar/=yaanga → भर्‍याङ.

07 / T and D sounds

Shift changes the tongue position

Lowercase t and d give dental sounds. Capital T and D give retroflex sounds. Keep the h lowercase for aspirated pairs.

DefaultWith Shift
ta तTa ट
tha थTha ठ
da दDa ड
dha धDha ढ
08 / More Shift sounds

Type ऋ, ष, and visarga

Capital R gives ऋ on its own or the ृ sign after a consonant. Lowercase ri and kri keep their ordinary sounds.

R→ऋ
kR→कृ
ri→रि
kri→क्रि

Capital S or Sh chooses ष; lowercase sh gives श. An optional a keeps the same full sound: Sa or Sha → ष, while sha → श.

Capital H after a vowel adds visarga ः. Lowercase h still types ह, and : stays an English colon.

Known words may keep their listed spelling when capitalized: Ram → राम and Sita → सीता.

kaH→कः
duHkha→दुःख
ha→ह
ka:→क:

14 / Nasal marks

Choose the mark explicitly

Type ^ after a syllable for बिन्दु ं, or ~ for चन्द्रबिन्दु ँ. The letters n and m still make consonants.

ka^→कं
kaa~→काँ
15 / Digits

Number keys type Nepali digits

In Nepali mode, number keys produce ०–९. Decimal points, commas, signs, and date separators keep their punctuation. Numbers inside recognized links and email addresses stay as typed. Pasted numbers follow the same rule.

0123456789→०१२३४५६७८९
3.14→३.१४

Try September 27, 2026 for सेप्टेम्बर २७, २०२६. This changes digit characters and month spelling; it does not change the number, year, or calendar. Switch to English mode to keep 0–9 from the first key. Existing text stays as it is.

16 / Punctuation

Period or पूर्णविराम

The . key always types an English period, including after a Nepali word and in decimals. Type | for । after Nepali text or a number. Inside a recognized link or email address, punctuation stays literal. Pasted text follows the same rule.

namaste.→नमस्ते.
namaste|→नमस्ते।
3.14→३.१४
3.14|→३.१४।
17 / Alternatives

Choose only when needed

Ambiguous words show a readings dropdown. The first reading appears in the editor; pick another from the dropdown or press Alt + 1, 2, and so on. Press Space to keep the displayed reading and finish the word.

kam→कम / काम
18 / Mixed text

Links and email stay readable

Recognizable links and ordinary ASCII email addresses stay as you typed them in Nepali mode, including case, digits, paths, and query strings. Try an HTTP(S) link, a www. address, a domain such as camera.com, or name+tag@example.com.

namaste camera.com name@example.com ↓ नमस्ते camera.com name@example.com

Preservation starts early: http:, https:, www., name@, and camera.c already stay Roman before the address is complete. The same rule applies to typing and paste. Before a cue appears, camera still becomes क्यामेरा, and a trailing period alone stays sentence punctuation. Use English mode before the first key to keep a fragment literal from its beginning.

During continuous typing, an address cue restores the current token to its original spelling, including any digits that had appeared in Nepali before the cue. A space finishes it. A pipe after a bare domain gives ।; a pipe within a URL path stays literal. Add a space before | to end a sentence after a URL path.

This uses address patterns and accepts unfinished addresses without checking whether they are valid or a domain exists. A dotted spelling such as pani.paani or its unfinished form pani.p also stays literal. Internationalized addresses and arbitrary English or code are outside this automatic handling; use English mode for those.

19 / Editor controls

Switch, copy, or clear

The mode button shows Nepali mode when conversion is enabled and English mode when typing is literal. It switches the editor and all three marked form fields together. Existing text stays as it is.

Use English mode to type or paste an English phrase or code fragment, then switch back to continue Nepali typing. In English mode, digits and shortcut keys such as ^, ~, `, /, and | stay literal. The . key stays a period in both modes.

Copy copies the main editor text; Clear starts that editor again. English notes and the email-only field always stay literal.
